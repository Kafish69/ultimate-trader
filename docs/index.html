Nothing reaches your broker until three signals are green.

Ultimate Trader is a Claude skill that runs TradingAgents, Vibe-Trading and the Day Trader pack as one workflow: research the idea, test the odds, size the trade, check the risk, then ask you. It trades on a paper account by default.

git clone https://github.com/Kafish69/ultimate-trader && cd ultimate-trader && python install.py

Three engines, each good at one job

Ask in plain words. The skill picks the engine that fits and passes the result to the next stage.

TradingAgents

Research for swing trades and longer

A team of AI analysts covers fundamentals, news, sentiment and the chart. A bull researcher and a bear researcher argue, a risk panel pushes back, and a portfolio manager gives the final rating.

  • Any Yahoo ticker, such as NVDA, 0700.HK or BTC-USD
  • Needs one LLM key, set as an environment variable
  • Saves a markdown report and an HTML page
TauricResearch/TradingAgents, Apache-2.0

Vibe-Trading

Backtests, factors and your own habits

Tests a rule over past data with costs, from 11 backtest engines and 29 data sources. It also holds 478 ready-made alphas, prices options, and reads your broker export to show where you chase, overtrade or hold losers.

  • Free data for US, Hong Kong and crypto
  • Runs inside Claude as an MCP server
  • 90 finance method skills, read only when needed
HKUDS/Vibe-Trading, MIT

Day Trader pack

The intraday trade, start to finish

Eleven skills scan thousands of charts, watch the news, time the entry, set the stop and target, replay the odds 10,000 times, size the position, check the risk and hand an approved order to your broker.

  • Plain Python, no API key, free public data
  • Alerts for your stop, your target and the closing bell
  • Earnings summaries and Congress trade filings
Jakeschincariol/day-trader-skill, MIT

The route a trade takes

Each stop reads the file the one before it wrote. Choose a stop to see what it does. The two marked stops are gates: the trade ends there unless they pass.

    Size a trade the way the skill does

    The starting numbers are a $60 account with a $2 loss limit, and a TSLA entry with a stop at the opening-range midpoint and a 2R target. Change anything and watch the verdict. Try a target of 366 to see a block.

    Fixed limits: position up to 80% of budget, order up to $48, 5% cash kept back, reward to risk at least 1.5, daily loss $4, 2 trades a day.

    What the trade could make or lose

    This takes the trade you just sized and shows the money. Set where you might exit, your win rate and how much the stock usually moves in a day. It then plays the rest of the session 10,000 times.

    The 10,000 sessions here are a random walk, not the real Time Machine. The real one replays the stock's own recent 5-minute bars on your computer (/trader-timemachine). Use this to feel the shape of the odds, then run the real one.

    Watch one trade play out

    This is a paper trade with fake money. The chart plays one session in 5-minute steps from your entry. It ends when the price touches your stop or your target, or at the close. The faint lines are 40 other ways the same day could have gone.

    Install

    You need Python 3.11 or newer and Claude Code. Everything stays on your machine. The installer backs up your Claude config before it changes it.

    1. Run the installer

      It copies the 12 skills, builds two Python environments (TradingAgents and Vibe-Trading need different library versions) and registers the Vibe-Trading MCP server.

      python install.py

    2. Put your keys in environment variables

      One LLM key for TradingAgents, and Alpaca paper keys for the broker step. Never paste keys into a chat. Reopen your terminal afterwards.

      setx ANTHROPIC_API_KEY "..."
      setx ALPACA_API_KEY "PK..." && setx ALPACA_SECRET_KEY "..."

    3. Check it, restart Claude Code, ask

      The doctor reports OK, WARN or FAIL for each piece and never prints a key. Then try the first command below.

      python scripts/doctor.py

    Set your own limits by copying config.example.json to trader/config.json. For the broker, add Alpaca's official MCP server in paper mode; /trader-broker walks you through it.

    Questions

    Does it trade real money?

    Not unless you change it. The config ships with paper trading on, and Alpaca's server defaults to the paper account. Going live is a deliberate change you make yourself, and every order still needs the three signals.

    Can Claude place a trade on its own?

    No. Only the broker step can lead to an order. It needs a risk PASS for that exact order, your typed yes, and your click on Claude's permission card.

    Why two Python environments?

    TradingAgents and Vibe-Trading pin different versions of shared libraries, so pip cannot install them together. Each gets its own environment, and the installer manages both.

    Does it work with $60?

    Yes, with fractional shares and a small loss limit, which is what the default config sets. Spreads and costs weigh more on a small account, so treat it as a place to learn.

    How accurate are the predictions?

    There are none. The Time Machine simulates a stock's recent bars, and a backtest describes the past. Odds close to a coin flip mean you pass. A history of fewer than about 30 trades is an anecdote.

    Where does the data come from?

    Free, unofficial sources: Yahoo Finance charts, Nasdaq Trader symbol lists, SEC EDGAR and the House Clerk. They can lag or rate-limit. Check price-critical numbers with your broker.

    Not financial advice. This is software that reads public data and does arithmetic. It cannot see the future and does not know your situation. Most people who day trade lose money.
