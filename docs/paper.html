What the skills say about the chart right now

Each row is a simple version of the real skill, run on this chart: Scanner, Entry timer (a close above the last 12 candles' high), Exit planner (stop at the middle of that range, target at 2R), Time Machine (1,000 simulated finishes), Budget, Risk and Alerts. The skills account above runs this same logic by itself. You can follow it or ignore it.

Rules from the Ultimate Trader setup, scaled to your cash: the most you can lose on one trade is the percent you set, a rolling loss limit of twice that stops new trades for a while, shares are sized from the stop, a position never exceeds 80% of cash, one position at a time, a 6-candle pause after each trade, and a trade that goes nowhere is closed after 40 candles. A fee of 0.03% is charged on each side. The chart is a made-up random price series with some drift, not a real stock, and the skills only ever see candles that have already closed. A result here says nothing about real markets. Practice only, not financial advice.
