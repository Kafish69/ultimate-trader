Choose your cash and a goal. You and the skills each start with that cash and trade the same chart, day after day, until one of you reaches the goal. The skills read every candle and trade by themselves. You click Buy and Sell yourself.

Both accounts start with the same cash and the same chart. Cash carries over from day to day, so wins and losses compound. At Max speed days fly by and only the skills trade; your account waits until you slow down.
